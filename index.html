★ FAMILY OWNED ★

Our Story

Since 1995, Coffee Cup has been proudly serving the Long Beach community with fresh, comforting breakfast and lunch favorites. For more than 25 years, we've become known for our flavorful breakfast dishes, including customer favorites like the Chicken Chile Verde Omelette, Enchilada Eggs, Mashed Potato Omelette, and David's Scramble.

Whether you're craving something spicy, something mild, or something sweet, there's always something delicious waiting for you. From pancakes and French toast to vegetarian options, sandwiches, burgers, and fresh salads, Coffee Cup is a place where everyone can find something they love.

We're grateful to be part of Long Beach's daily routine and hope to welcome you soon for a meal that feels like home.

— The Coffee Cup Family
